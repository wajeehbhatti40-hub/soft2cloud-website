Computer Kanda

Computer Kanda turns a weighing scale into a billing counter. Weight times price shows on the front and rear displays, products have Urdu names and hot keys, and every sale lands in the daily report.

Features

Everything Computer Kanda does.

Weighing

  • Weight × price per kg
  • Tare for crates and bags
  • Piece counting
  • Check-weighing with limits

Products

  • Urdu and English names
  • Hot keys for fast items
  • Rate changes in seconds
  • Per kg or per piece

Billing

  • Several items on one bill

Questions about Computer Kanda

Do I need a special scale?

It works with digital scales that send weight to a PC, and has a manual mode for any scale.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The live demo is free and open to everyone with sample data. For a trial with your own data, contact us.

Do you give support?

Yes. Help on WhatsApp and email from the team that built the software, plus updates while your licence is active.
