Mart POS

Mart POS is a point-of-sale for supermarkets and general stores. Cashiers scan and bill in seconds, stock goes down by itself, and the owner sees sales, profit and stock from any screen.

Features

Everything Mart POS does.

Fast billing

  • Barcode and QR scanning
  • Discounts per item or bill
  • Quick product search
  • Thermal receipt with QR

Stock and products

  • Auto category for new products
  • Low-stock alerts
  • Purchase entries from suppliers
  • Expiry dates

Cash control

  • Counter opening balance
  • Closing with Z-report
  • Cash, card and wallet payments
  • Returns and refunds with a reason

Bills and returns

  • Hold and recall bills
  • Reprint any old bill
  • Returns linked to the original bill
  • Bill search by number or date

Logins and roles

  • Admin, accountant and cashier
  • Cashier cannot change prices
  • Every bill linked to the cashier
  • Activity log

Reports

  • Daily, monthly and item-wise sales
  • Profit by product and category
  • Stock value
  • Export to Excel

Questions about Mart POS

Which barcode scanners work?

Any USB or Bluetooth scanner that types like a keyboard works, as do most phone cameras for QR.

Can I use it at more than one counter?

Yes. Each counter has its own cashier login and closing report.

Can it print on a thermal printer?

Yes, 58 mm and 80 mm receipt printers are supported.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The live demo is free and open to everyone with sample data. For a trial with your own data, contact us.

Do you give support?

Yes. Help on WhatsApp and email from the team that built the software, plus updates while your licence is active.
