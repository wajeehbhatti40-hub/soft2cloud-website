Weighbridge

Weighbridge software records every vehicle that crosses your kanta: first and second weight, net load, driver and vehicle details, and the fee — then prints a clean slip and keeps the daily report.

For: Dharam kanta, mills, factories, mandis

Features

Everything Weighbridge does.

Weighing

  • First and second weight
  • Automatic net load
  • Stored tare for regular vehicles
  • Manual or indicator input

Vehicles and drivers

  • Vehicle number and type
  • Driver name and licence
  • Party and material
  • Repeat vehicles filled in automatically

Slips

  • Printed slip with serial number
  • Duplicate copy on demand
  • Weights in kg and maund
  • Your business name on top

Fees

  • Fee by vehicle type
  • Fees totalled per day
  • Party-wise records
  • Daily cash report

Reports

  • Daily and monthly weighings
  • By party, material and vehicle
  • Pending second weights list
  • Printable reports

Control

  • Operator logins
  • Weighbridge indicator input
  • Backups
  • Runs on a PC at the kanta

Questions about Weighbridge

Can it read the weight from my indicator?

Yes, most weighbridge indicators with a serial or USB output can be connected. We check your model during setup.

Does it show maund?

Yes. Slips can show kilograms and maund (40 kg).

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The live demo is free and open to everyone with sample data. For a trial with your own data, contact us.

Do you give support?

Yes. Help on WhatsApp and email from the team that built the software, plus updates while your licence is active.
