VenueDesk

VenueDesk runs a wedding hall or banquet from first enquiry to the last payment. Every booking sits on a live calendar that blocks double-booking, every rupee is tracked against the event, and your staff see only the work that is theirs.

For: Wedding halls, banquets, marquees, farmhouses

Features

Everything VenueDesk does.

Calendar and availability

  • Halls, lawns and marquees side by side
  • Lunch and dinner shifts
  • Hard lock on double-booking
  • Tentative holds that expire

Enquiries to bookings

  • Enquiry list with follow-up dates
  • Packages per head with menus
  • Add-ons: stage, lights, DJ, decor
  • Convert an enquiry to a booking in one click

Payments and invoices

  • Advance, instalments and balance
  • Printed receipts and invoices
  • Outstanding list by event date
  • Expense and vendor payments

Staff alerts and tasks

  • Tasks per event for each staff member
  • Alerts for upcoming events
  • Staff see only their own work
  • Checklists for setup and closing

Logins and control

  • Owner, manager, accountant, staff roles
  • Name and PIN sign-in
  • Actions hidden from roles that should not see them
  • Full history of changes

Reports and backup

  • Monthly bookings and income
  • Hall occupancy
  • Download a full backup any time
  • Urdu and English

Questions about VenueDesk

Can I run more than one hall?

Yes. Add every hall, lawn or marquee you have and book them by shift.

Does it work on phone?

Yes. Owners and managers often check the calendar on their phone while staff use a PC at the front desk.

Can I move my old bookings?

Yes. We can load your existing bookings and pending payments during setup.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The live demo is free and open to everyone with sample data. For a trial with your own data, contact us.

Do you give support?

Yes. Help on WhatsApp and email from the team that built the software, plus updates while your licence is active.
