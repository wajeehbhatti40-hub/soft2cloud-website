Questions about School Management System

Can parents see results on their phone?

Yes. Parents get their own login to see attendance, homework, results and fee status.

Can we import our students?

Yes, from Excel — a whole class takes about a minute.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The live demo is free and open to everyone with sample data. For a trial with your own data, contact us.

Do you give support?

Yes. Help on WhatsApp and email from the team that built the software, plus updates while your licence is active.
