School Management Software: 10 Features That Actually Matter

Many schools buy software for its long feature list and end up using three screens. These are the features that save time every single day.

The ten that matter

  1. Fee vouchers and receipts — monthly vouchers, receipts on payment, and a clear defaulter list.
  2. Daily attendance by teachers — on a phone, in under a minute per class.
  3. Parent access — parents see attendance, homework, results and fees on their own phone.
  4. Homework and diary — no more lost notebooks.
  5. Marks entry and report cards — teachers enter marks, the system works out totals, grades and positions.
  6. Notices — one message to every parent, or to one class.
  7. Fast data entry — import a whole class from Excel instead of typing each student.
  8. Separate logins — principal, accountant, teacher, parent and student each see their own part.
  9. Urdu and English — for staff and parents who are more comfortable in Urdu.
  10. Local support — someone who answers on WhatsApp during admissions and exam weeks.

Questions to ask before you buy

  • Who owns the data, and can we download a full backup?
  • Does it work on cheap Android phones?
  • What does it cost per year, and does the price grow with the number of students?
  • How long does setup take, and will you train our teachers?

Ask for a demo with your own classes loaded. If a teacher can mark attendance and enter marks without help after ten minutes, the software will actually be used.
