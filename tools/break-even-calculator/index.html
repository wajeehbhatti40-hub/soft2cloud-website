Break-even Calculator

Find the number of units you must sell each month before you start making a profit, and how many you need for a profit target. Useful before opening a shop or launching a product.

How to use the Break-even Calculator

  1. Add up fixed monthly costs: rent, salaries, bills, instalments.
  2. Enter the selling price per unit.
  3. Enter the variable cost per unit (stock, packing, commission).
  4. Optionally set a monthly profit target.

Formula

Contribution per unit = Price − Variable cost Break-even units = Fixed costs ÷ Contribution per unit

Example

With Rs 300,000 fixed costs, a price of Rs 500 and a variable cost of Rs 300, each sale contributes Rs 200, so you need 1,500 units a month (about 50 a day) to break even.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a fixed cost?

Anything you pay whether you sell or not: rent, staff salaries, internet, loan instalments, software licences.

Results are estimates for planning. For tax, legal or religious decisions, confirm with a qualified person. Your inputs stay in your browser and are not sent to Soft2Cloud.
