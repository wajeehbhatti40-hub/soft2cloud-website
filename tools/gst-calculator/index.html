GST / Sales Tax Calculator

Work out sales tax on a bill in one step. Enter an amount, pick whether it already includes tax, and see the amount before tax, the GST and the total. The rate starts at 18% and you can change it for any other rate.

How to use the GST / Sales Tax Calculator

  1. Type the amount in rupees.
  2. Check or change the tax rate (18% is filled in).
  3. Choose “Before tax” to add GST, or “Including tax” to take it out.
  4. Read the tax and total in the blue box — it updates as you type.

Formula

Add GST: Tax = Amount × Rate ÷ 100, Total = Amount + Tax Remove GST: Amount = Total ÷ (1 + Rate ÷ 100), Tax = Total − Amount

Example

A supplier quotes Rs 50,000 before tax. At 18%, the GST is Rs 9,000 and the bill is Rs 59,000. If a bill of Rs 59,000 already includes 18% tax, the price without tax is Rs 50,000.

Frequently asked questions

What is the sales tax rate in Pakistan?

The standard federal sales tax on goods is 18%, but reduced, zero and higher rates apply to some items, and services are taxed by the provinces at their own rates. Always confirm the current rate with FBR or your provincial revenue authority.

Is GST the same as sales tax?

In everyday use, yes. People in Pakistan say GST for the general sales tax charged on a sale.

Can I use this for other countries?

Yes. Just type the VAT or GST rate of that country.
