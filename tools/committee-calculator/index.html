Committee (Kamati) Calculator

Plan a committee (kamati, BC) with friends or colleagues. See the pot size, how long it runs, what you will have paid when your turn comes and the full month-by-month schedule.

How to use the Committee (Kamati) Calculator

  1. Enter the monthly contribution.
  2. Enter the number of members (the committee runs that many months).
  3. Enter your turn number.
  4. Pick the first month to see real dates.

Formula

Pot = Monthly amount × Members Paid by your turn = Monthly amount × Your turn

Example

A Rs 20,000 committee with 10 members gives a pot of Rs 200,000. If your turn is third, you have paid Rs 60,000 when you receive it — like an interest-free loan of Rs 140,000.

Frequently asked questions

Is an early or late turn better?

An early turn is like a loan; a late turn is like saving. In a fair committee no one gains or loses money, only timing changes.

Results are estimates for planning. For tax, legal or religious decisions, confirm with a qualified person. Your inputs stay in your browser and are not sent to Soft2Cloud.
