Case Converter

Fix text typed in the wrong case, or format names and headings in one click. Includes developer formats like camelCase and snake_case.

How to use the Case Converter

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Choose a case.
  3. Copy the result.

Example

“SOFT2CLOUD MAKES SOFTWARE” in Sentence case becomes “Soft2cloud makes software”.

Results are estimates for planning. For tax, legal or religious decisions, confirm with a qualified person. Your inputs stay in your browser and are not sent to Soft2Cloud.
