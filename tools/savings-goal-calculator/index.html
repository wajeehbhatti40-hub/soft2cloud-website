Savings Goal Calculator

Planning for a wedding, Hajj, a car or school fees? Enter the target and the date, and find out how much to put aside every month.

How to use the Savings Goal Calculator

  1. Enter the goal amount.
  2. Enter what you have already saved.
  3. Enter the number of years and an expected return (0 if you keep cash).

Formula

Monthly saving = (Goal − Savings × (1+i)^m) × i ÷ ((1+i)^m − 1) i = yearly return ÷ 12 ÷ 100, m = months

Example

To reach Rs 1.5 million in 3 years with Rs 200,000 already saved and a 10% return, you need about Rs 29,450 a month.

Frequently asked questions

What if I keep the money at home?

Set the return to 0. The tool then divides what you still need by the number of months.

Results are estimates for planning. For tax, legal or religious decisions, confirm with a qualified person. Your inputs stay in your browser and are not sent to Soft2Cloud.
