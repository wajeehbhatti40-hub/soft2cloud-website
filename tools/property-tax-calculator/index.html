Property Transfer Tax Calculator

Work out the government charges when buying or selling a house or plot in Pakistan: FBR advance tax on the buyer (236K) and seller (236C) for filers and non-filers, plus provincial stamp duty and other fees.

How to use the Property Transfer Tax Calculator

  1. Enter the property value — use the FBR or DC value if it is higher than the price.
  2. Choose whether the buyer and seller are filers.
  3. Check the stamp duty for your province and add registration or society fees.

Formula

Buyer: 236K = value × (1.25% filer, or 10.5% / 14.5% / 18.5% non-filer) Seller: 236C = value × (2.75% filer, or 11.5% non-filer) Stamp duty = value × provincial rate

Example

For a Rs 15 million house in Lahore with both parties filers: the buyer pays Rs 187,500 (236K) + Rs 150,000 stamp duty, and the seller pays Rs 412,500 (236C).

Frequently asked questions

Is 236C the same as capital gains tax?

No. 236C is an advance tax collected at transfer. Capital gains tax is worked out separately on the profit in your tax return.

What is the stamp duty in Punjab?

Punjab set a uniform 1% stamp duty on property in April 2026. Other provinces use different rates, and registration and society fees are extra.

Do overseas Pakistanis pay non-filer rates?

Non-resident Pakistanis have a separate facility and do not pay the non-filer rates. Check with FBR for the documents needed.
