Invoice Generator

Create a clean invoice with your business details, items, discount and tax, with the total written in words. Print it or save it as a PDF from the print window. Your details stay saved in this browser for next time.

How to use the Invoice Generator

  1. Fill in your business details once — they are remembered.
  2. Add the customer and the invoice number and dates.
  3. Add each item with quantity and rate.
  4. Add discount or tax if needed.
  5. Press “Print / Save as PDF” and choose “Save as PDF” as the printer.

Formula

Subtotal = Σ (Qty × Rate) Total = (Subtotal − Discount) × (1 + Tax ÷ 100)

Example

Three items of Rs 1,000, one item of Rs 2,500 and 18% tax gives a subtotal of Rs 5,500, tax of Rs 990 and a total of Rs 6,490.
