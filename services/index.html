Common questions

How long does a custom project take?

Small tools take one to three weeks. Larger systems with several modules usually take one to three months. You get a timeline with the quote.

Who owns the software?

This is agreed in the quote. Usually you get a licence for your business; full source ownership can be included.

Can you change one of your ready products for us?

Yes. Most customers start from a ready product and we add what is missing — this is faster and cheaper than starting from zero.
