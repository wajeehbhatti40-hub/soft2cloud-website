About Soft2Cloud

A Lahore software house that ships working products.

Soft2Cloud builds business software for every size of business: a small office, a petrol pump, a supermarket, a school or a multinational. We start from the way your staff already work, and we put it on the screens they already have.

Every Soft2Cloud product runs on a licence for your business, comes in Urdu and English, and is backed by our own team. No outsourcing and no copied templates. We also publish free online tools because many small businesses in Pakistan still do their sums by hand — and that is how many of our customers first find us.
