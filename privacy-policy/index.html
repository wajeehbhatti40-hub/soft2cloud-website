Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

What we collect

This website does not have accounts and does not store the messages you write in the contact form. When you press “Send on WhatsApp” or “Send by email”, your message opens in your own WhatsApp or email app and you decide whether to send it.

Free tools

All free tools run inside your browser. Numbers, text, documents and images you enter are not sent to Soft2Cloud. Some tools save a draft in your browser’s local storage so it is there when you come back; you can clear it by clearing your browser data.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, and the contact page shows a Google Maps map. The currency converter fetches exchange rates from open.er-api.com, and the gold and zakat tools fetch approximate metal prices from gold-api.com. These services may see your IP address as part of a normal web request. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Software customers

Data you keep in Soft2Cloud software you license is handled under the agreement for that software.
